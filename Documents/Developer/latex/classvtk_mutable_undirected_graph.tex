\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
An editable undirected graph. 

\doxylink{classvtk_mutable_undirected_graph}{vtk\+Mutable\+Undirected\+Graph} is an undirected graph with additional functions for adding vertices and edges. \doxylink{classvtk_graph_a981281cff554790bee0bdf05fb236540}{Shallow\+Copy()}, \doxylink{classvtk_graph_aac4d6daae82193ac6c5835225677a0e1}{Deep\+Copy()}, \doxylink{classvtk_graph_a93c80d654a851b754342cc0f81a4ad28}{Checked\+Shallow\+Copy()}, and \doxylink{classvtk_graph_a3733a589dd1446186715259671912b10}{Checked\+Deep\+Copy()} will succeed when the argument is a \doxylink{classvtk_undirected_graph}{vtk\+Undirected\+Graph} or \doxylink{classvtk_mutable_undirected_graph}{vtk\+Mutable\+Undirected\+Graph}.
