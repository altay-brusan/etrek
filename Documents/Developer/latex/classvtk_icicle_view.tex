\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Displays a tree in a stacked "{}icicle"{} view. 

\doxylink{classvtk_icicle_view}{vtk\+Icicle\+View} shows a \doxylink{classvtk_tree}{vtk\+Tree} in horizontal layers where each vertex in the tree is represented by a bar. Child sectors are below (or above) parent sectors, and may be colored and sized by various parameters.
