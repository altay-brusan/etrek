\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Interpolates supplied nodes with bezier line segments. 

\doxylink{requirements_The}{The} line interpolator interpolates supplied nodes (see Interpolate\+Line) with Bezier line segments. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} fitness of the curve may be controlled using Set\+Maximum\+Curve\+Error and Set\+Maximum\+Number\+Of\+Line\+Segments.
