\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A direction encoder based on spherical coordinates. 

\doxylink{classvtk_spherical_direction_encoder}{vtk\+Spherical\+Direction\+Encoder} is a direction encoder which uses spherical coordinates for mapping (nx, ny, nz) into an azimuth, elevation pair.
