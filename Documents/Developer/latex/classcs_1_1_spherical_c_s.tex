\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A three-\/dimensional coordinate system in Euclidean space with one distance measured from the origin and two angular coordinates. 

Not to be confused with an ellipsoidal coordinate system based on an ellipsoid "{}degenerated"{} into a sphere. A \doxylink{classcs_1_1_spherical_c_s}{Spherical\+CS} shall have three axis associations.
