\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
the animation scene manager. 

\doxylink{classvtk_animation_cue}{vtk\+Animation\+Cue} and \doxylink{classvtk_animation_scene}{vtk\+Animation\+Scene} provide the framework to support animations in VTK. \doxylink{classvtk_animation_cue}{vtk\+Animation\+Cue} represents an entity that changes/\+ animates with time, while \doxylink{classvtk_animation_scene}{vtk\+Animation\+Scene} represents scene or setup for the animation, which consists of individual cues or other scenes.

A scene can be played in real time mode, or as a sequence of frames 1/\+frame rate apart in time. \begin{DoxySeeAlso}{See also}
