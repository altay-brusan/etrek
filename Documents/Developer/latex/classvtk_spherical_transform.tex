\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
spherical to rectangular coords and back 

\doxylink{classvtk_spherical_transform}{vtk\+Spherical\+Transform} will convert (r,phi,theta) coordinates to (x,y,z) coordinates and back again. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} angles are given in radians. By default, it converts spherical coordinates to rectangular, but \doxylink{classvtk_abstract_transform_aa3537b1f7129e6e5bf0807eded5215ce}{Get\+Inverse()} returns a transform that will do the opposite. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} equation that is used is x = r\texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*}sin(phi)\texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*}cos(theta), y = r\texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*}sin(phi)\texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*}sin(theta), z = r\texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*}cos(phi). \begin{DoxyWarning}{Warning}
This transform is not well behaved along the line x=y=0 (i.\+e. along the z-\/axis) 
\end{DoxyWarning}
