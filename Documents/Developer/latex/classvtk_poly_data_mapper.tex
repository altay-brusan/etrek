\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
map \doxylink{classvtk_poly_data}{vtk\+Poly\+Data} to graphics primitives 

\doxylink{classvtk_poly_data_mapper}{vtk\+Poly\+Data\+Mapper} is a class that maps polygonal data (i.\+e., \doxylink{classvtk_poly_data}{vtk\+Poly\+Data}) to graphics primitives. \doxylink{classvtk_poly_data_mapper}{vtk\+Poly\+Data\+Mapper} serves as a superclass for device-\/specific poly data mappers, that actually do the mapping to the rendering/\+graphics hardware/\+software.

Methods for VBO coordinate shift+scale-\/computation. 

By default, shift and scale vectors are enabled whenever Create\+VBO is called with points whose bounds are many bbox-\/lengths away from the origin.

Shifting and scaling may be completely disabled, or manually specified, or left at the default.

Manual specification is for the case when you will be calling Append\+VBO instead of just Create\+VBO and know better bounds than the what Create\+VBO might produce.

\doxylink{requirements_The}{The} automatic method tells Creat\+VBO to compute shift and scale vectors that remap the points to the unit cube.
