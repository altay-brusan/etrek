\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Iterates through adjacent vertices in a graph. 

\doxylink{classvtk_adjacent_vertex_iterator}{vtk\+Adjacent\+Vertex\+Iterator} iterates through all vertices adjacent to a vertex, i.\+e. the vertices which may be reached by traversing an out edge of the source vertex. Use graph-\/\texorpdfstring{$>$}{>}Get\+Adjacent\+Vertices(v, it) to initialize the iterator.
