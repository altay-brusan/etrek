\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
superclass for all geometric transformations 

\doxylink{classvtk_abstract_transform}{vtk\+Abstract\+Transform} is the superclass for all VTK geometric transformations. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} VTK transform hierarchy is split into two major branches:\+ warp transformations and homogeneous (including linear) transformations. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} latter can be represented in terms of a 4x4 transformation matrix, the former cannot. 

Transformations can be pipelined through two mechanisms:\+ 

1) \doxylink{classvtk_abstract_transform_aa3537b1f7129e6e5bf0807eded5215ce}{Get\+Inverse()} returns the pipelined inverse of a transformation i.\+e. if you modify the original transform, any transform previously returned by the \doxylink{classvtk_abstract_transform_aa3537b1f7129e6e5bf0807eded5215ce}{Get\+Inverse()} method will automatically update itself according to the change. 

2) You can do pipelined concatenation of transformations through the \doxylink{classvtk_general_transform}{vtk\+General\+Transform} class, the \doxylink{classvtk_perspective_transform}{vtk\+Perspective\+Transform} class, or the \doxylink{classvtk_transform}{vtk\+Transform} class. \begin{DoxySeeAlso}{See also}
