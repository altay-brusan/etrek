\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Perform calculations (mostly intersection calculations) on regions of a 3D binary spatial partitioning. 

Given an axis aligned binary spatial partitioning described by a \doxylink{classvtk_b_s_p_cuts}{vtk\+BSPCuts} object, perform intersection queries on various geometric entities with regions of the spatial partitioning.
