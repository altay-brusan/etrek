\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
An ordered sequence of two or more single coordinate operations (\doxylink{classoperation_1_1_single_operation}{Single\+Operation}). 

\doxylink{requirements_The}{The} sequence of coordinate operations is constrained by the requirement that the source coordinate reference system of step n+1 shall be the same as the target coordinate reference system of step n.
