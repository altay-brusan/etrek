\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Records held by a hash-\/entry that represent the side of one cell.

All instances of \doxylink{structvtk_cell_grid_sides_cache_1_1_side}{Side} owned by a single hash-\/entry have the same hash but correspond to distinct sides of different cells.
