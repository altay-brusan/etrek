\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
an ordered list of Props 

\doxylink{classvtk_prop_collection}{vtk\+Prop\+Collection} represents and provides methods to manipulate a list of Props (i.\+e., \doxylink{classvtk_prop}{vtk\+Prop} and subclasses). \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} list is ordered and duplicate entries are not prevented.
