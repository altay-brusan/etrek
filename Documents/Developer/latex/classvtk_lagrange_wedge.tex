\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A 3D cell that represents an arbitrary order Lagrange wedge. 

\doxylink{classvtk_lagrange_wedge}{vtk\+Lagrange\+Wedge} is a concrete implementation of \doxylink{classvtk_cell}{vtk\+Cell} to represent a 3D wedge using Lagrange shape functions of user specified order. A wedge consists of two triangular and three quadrilateral faces. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} first six points of the wedge (0-\/5) are the "{}corner"{} points where the first three points are the base of the wedge. This wedge point ordering is opposite the \doxylink{classvtk_wedge}{vtk\+Wedge} ordering though in that the base of the wedge defined by the first three points (0,1,2) form a triangle whose normal points inward (toward the triangular face (3,4,5)). While this is opposite the \doxylink{classvtk_wedge}{vtk\+Wedge} convention it is consistent with every other cell type in VTK. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} first 2 parametric coordinates of the Lagrange wedge or for the triangular base and vary between 0 and 1. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} third parametric coordinate is between the two triangular faces and goes from 0 to 1 as well.
