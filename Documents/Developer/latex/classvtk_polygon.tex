\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
a cell that represents an n-\/sided polygon 

\doxylink{classvtk_polygon}{vtk\+Polygon} is a concrete implementation of \doxylink{classvtk_cell}{vtk\+Cell} to represent a 2D n-\/sided polygon. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} polygons cannot have any internal holes, and cannot self-\/intersect. Define the polygon with n-\/points ordered in the counter-\/ clockwise direction; do not repeat the last point.

\doxylink{requirements_The}{The} strategy used is to compute the average coordinate x\+\_\+c (the center, but not the centroid of the polygon) and then apply the "{}geometric decomposition"{} method for centroids to an area-\/weighted sum centroids of triangles formed from the center x\+\_\+c to each edge of the polygon.
