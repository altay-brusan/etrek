Detailed Description

Display polar axes in Viewport 2D space.

The polar axes actor is drawn on overlay. It displays polar coordinates. It is made of concentric axes linked with arcs.

Warning
Please be aware that the axes coordinate values are subject to perspective effects. With perspective projection, the computed distances may look wrong. These effects are not present when parallel projection is enabled. (
See also
vtkCamera::ParallelProjectionOn())

It is the polar counter part of vtkLegendScaleActor.

See also
vtkRadialGridActor2D
