\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\doxylink{requirements_The}{The} definition of the origin and orientation of an engineering coordinate reference system. 

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
\doxylink{requirements_The}{The} origin can be fixed with respect to the Earth (such as a defined point at a construction site), or be a defined point on a moving vehicle (such as on a ship or satellite), or a defined point of an image.
\end{DoxyNote}
