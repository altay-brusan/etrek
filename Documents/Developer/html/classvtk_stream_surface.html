Detailed Description

Advect a stream surface in a vector field.

vtkStreamSurface is a surface streamer that generates a surface using the vectors in the input. Depending on the UseIterativeSeeding parameter, the simple or iterative version is called. The iterative version produces better surfaces, but takes longer.
