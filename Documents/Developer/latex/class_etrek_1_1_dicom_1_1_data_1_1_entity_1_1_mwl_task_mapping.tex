\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Maps MWL entries to the DICOM objects created during examination. 

This entity links a worklist entry and procedure to all the DICOM records created during an examination:\+ study, series, images, SOP common, and acquisition. This enables tracking the complete chain from worklist to final images.
