\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Visualization data composed of cells of arbitrary type. 

\doxylink{classvtk_cell_grid}{vtk\+Cell\+Grid} inherits \doxylink{classvtk_data_object}{vtk\+Data\+Object} in order to introduce the concept of cells that, instead of relying on spatial points to specify their shape, rely on degrees of freedom (which may or may not be embedded in a world coordinate system).

\doxylink{requirements_The}{The} degrees of freedom that define cells and the functions using those cells as their domain are provided in data arrays. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} arrays are partitioned into groups (\doxylink{classvtk_data_set_attributes}{vtk\+Data\+Set\+Attributes}) by the registered cell types. Each array in a group has the same number of tuples.
