\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Picks an entire viewport at one shot. 

\doxylink{requirements_The}{The} Scene picker, unlike conventional pickers picks an entire viewport at one shot and caches the result, which can be retrieved later. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} utility of the class arises during {\bfseries{Actor Selection}}. Let\textquotesingle{}s say you have a couple of polygonal objects in your scene and you wish to have a status bar that indicates the object your mouse is over. Picking repeatedly every time your mouse moves would be very slow. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} scene picker automatically picks your viewport every time the camera is changed and caches the information. Additionally, it observes the \doxylink{classvtk_render_window_interactor}{vtk\+Render\+Window\+Interactor} to avoid picking during interaction, so that you still maintain your interactivity. In effect, the picker does an additional pick-\/render of your scene every time you stop interacting with your scene. As an example, see Rendering/\+\+Test\+Scene\+Picker.

\begin{DoxyWarning}{Warning}

\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Unlike a \doxylink{classvtk_hover_widget}{vtk\+Hover\+Widget}, this class is not timer based. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} hover widget picks a scene when the mouse is over an actor for a specified duration.
