\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Compute the histogram for an image. 

\doxylink{classvtk_image_histogram}{vtk\+Image\+Histogram} generates a histogram from its input, and optionally produces a 2D black-\/and-\/white image of the histogram as its output. Unlike the class \doxylink{classvtk_image_accumulate}{vtk\+Image\+Accumulate}, a multi-\/component image does not result in a multi-\/dimensional histogram. Instead, the resulting histogram will be the sum of the histograms of each of the individual components, unless Set\+Active\+Component is used to choose a single component. \begin{DoxyParagraph}{Thanks:\+}
Thanks to David Gobbi at the Seaman Family MR Centre and Dept. of Clinical Neurosciences, Foothills Medical Centre, Calgary, for providing this class. 
\end{DoxyParagraph}
