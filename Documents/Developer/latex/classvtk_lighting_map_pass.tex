Renders lighting information directly instead of final shaded colors. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} information keys allow the selection of either normal rendering or luminance. For normals, the (nx, ny, nz) tuple are rendered directly into the (r,g,b) fragment. For luminance, the diffuse and specular intensities are rendered into the red and green channels, respectively. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} blue channel is zero. For both luminances and normals, the alpha channel is set to 1.\+0 if present.
