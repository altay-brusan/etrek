\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A 3D representation for \doxylink{classvtk_camera_orientation_widget}{vtk\+Camera\+Orientation\+Widget}. 

Hover over the representation and drag with LMB to orbit around the view. Clicking on one of the axis labels will snap to that view. Click again on the same axis to switch to the opposite view of that same axis.

\doxylink{requirements_The}{The} representation anchors itself to a corner of the renderer\textquotesingle{}s viewport. See Anchor\+Type.
