\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
superclass for 3D geometric pickers (uses ray cast) 

\doxylink{classvtk_picker}{vtk\+Picker} is used to select instances of \doxylink{classvtk_prop3_d}{vtk\+Prop3D} by shooting a ray into a graphics window and intersecting with the actor\textquotesingle{}s bounding box. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} ray is defined from a point defined in window (or pixel) coordinates, and a point located from the camera\textquotesingle{}s position.

\doxylink{classvtk_picker}{vtk\+Picker} may return more than one \doxylink{classvtk_prop3_d}{vtk\+Prop3D}, since more than one bounding box may be intersected. \doxylink{classvtk_picker}{vtk\+Picker} returns an unsorted list of props that were hit, and a list of the corresponding world points of the hits. For the \doxylink{classvtk_prop3_d}{vtk\+Prop3D} that is closest to the camera, \doxylink{classvtk_picker}{vtk\+Picker} returns the pick coordinates in world and untransformed mapper space, the prop itself, the data set, and the mapper. For \doxylink{classvtk_picker}{vtk\+Picker} the closest prop is the one whose center point (i.\+e., center of bounding box) projected on the view ray is closest to the camera. Subclasses of \doxylink{classvtk_picker}{vtk\+Picker} use other methods for computing the pick point.

\begin{DoxySeeAlso}{See also}
\doxylink{classvtk_picker}{vtk\+Picker} is used for quick geometric picking. If you desire more precise picking of points or cells based on the geometry of any \doxylink{classvtk_prop3_d}{vtk\+Prop3D}, use the subclasses \doxylink{classvtk_point_picker}{vtk\+Point\+Picker} or \doxylink{classvtk_cell_picker}{vtk\+Cell\+Picker}. For hardware-\/accelerated picking of any \doxylink{struct_o_fremove__volatile}{type} of \doxylink{classvtk_prop}{vtk\+Prop}, use \doxylink{classvtk_prop_picker}{vtk\+Prop\+Picker} or \doxylink{classvtk_world_point_picker}{vtk\+World\+Point\+Picker}. 
\end{DoxySeeAlso}
