\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
a linear transform specified by two corresponding point sets 

A \doxylink{classvtk_landmark_transform}{vtk\+Landmark\+Transform} is defined by two sets of landmarks, the transform computed gives the best fit mapping one onto the other, in a least squares sense. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} indices are taken to correspond, so point 1 in the first set will get mapped close to point 1 in the second set, etc. Call Set\+Source\+Landmarks and Set\+Target\+Landmarks to specify the two sets of landmarks, ensure they have the same number of points. \begin{DoxyWarning}{Warning}
Whenever you add, subtract, or set points you must call \doxylink{classvtk_abstract_transform_a0b8b9fbcda89ba3ddb6f186762b1d0d9}{Modified()} on the \doxylink{classvtk_points}{vtk\+Points} object, or the transformation might not update. 
\end{DoxyWarning}
