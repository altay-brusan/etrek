\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A two-\/ or three-\/dimensional coordinate system in Euclidean space with orthogonal straight axes. 

All axes shall have the same length unit. A \doxylink{classcs_1_1_cartesian_c_s}{Cartesian\+CS} shall have two or three axis associations; the number of associations shall equal the dimension of the CS.
