\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}

\doxylink{classvtk_closest_point_strategy}{vtk\+Closest\+Point\+Strategy} is implements a \doxylink{classvtk_closest_point_strategy_a04bffe70e377eaeb8d1d4a07465af74d}{Find\+Cell()} strategy based on locating the closest point in a dataset, and then searching the attached cells. While relatively fast, it does not always return the correct result (it may not find a cell, since the closest cell may not be connected to the closest point). \doxylink{classvtk_cell_locator_strategy}{vtk\+Cell\+Locator\+Strategy} or \doxylink{classvtk_closest_n_points_strategy}{vtk\+Closest\+NPoints\+Strategy} will produce better results at the cost of speed.
