\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\doxylink{requirements_The}{The} representation for a \doxylink{classvtk_implicit_frustum_widget}{vtk\+Implicit\+Frustum\+Widget}. 

This class is a concrete representation for the \doxylink{classvtk_implicit_frustum_widget}{vtk\+Implicit\+Frustum\+Widget}. It represents an infinite frustum defined by its origin, its orientation, the two angles between its forward axis and its horizontal and vertical planes, and the distance between its origin and near plane. This frustum representation can be manipulated by using the \doxylink{classvtk_implicit_frustum_widget}{vtk\+Implicit\+Frustum\+Widget}.
