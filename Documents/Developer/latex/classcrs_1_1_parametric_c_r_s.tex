\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Contextually local coordinate reference system associated with an engineering datum. 

This is applied either to activities on or near the surface of the Earth without geodetic corrections, or on moving platforms such as road vehicles vessels, aircraft or spacecraft, or as the internal \doxylink{classcrs_1_1_c_r_s}{CRS} of an image.
