\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Advect a stream surface in a vector field. 

\doxylink{classvtk_stream_surface}{vtk\+Stream\+Surface} is a surface streamer that generates a surface using the vectors in the input. Depending on the Use\+Iterative\+Seeding parameter, the simple or iterative version is called. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} iterative version produces better surfaces, but takes longer.
