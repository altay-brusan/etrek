\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
a subclass of \doxylink{classvtk_prop3_d_follower}{vtk\+Prop3\+DFollower} that ensures that data is always parallel to the axis defined by a \doxylink{classvtk_axis_actor}{vtk\+Axis\+Actor}. 

\doxylink{classvtk_prop3_d_axis_follower}{vtk\+Prop3\+DAxis\+Follower} is a subclass of \doxylink{classvtk_prop3_d_follower}{vtk\+Prop3\+DFollower} that always follows its specified axis. More specifically it will not change its position or scale, but it will continually update its orientation so that it is aligned with the axis and facing at angle to the camera to provide maximum visibility. This is typically used for text labels for 3d plots. \begin{DoxySeeAlso}{See also}
