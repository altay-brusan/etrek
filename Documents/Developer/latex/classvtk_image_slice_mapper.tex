\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
map a slice of a \doxylink{classvtk_image_data}{vtk\+Image\+Data} to the screen 

\doxylink{classvtk_image_slice_mapper}{vtk\+Image\+Slice\+Mapper} is a mapper that will draw a 2D image, or a slice of a 3D image. For 3D images, the slice may be oriented in the X, Y, or Z direction. This mapper works via 2D textures with accelerated zoom and pan operations. \begin{DoxyParagraph}{Thanks:\+}
Thanks to David Gobbi at the Seaman Family MR Centre and Dept. of Clinical Neurosciences, Foothills Medical Centre, Calgary, for providing this class.
