\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Converts 2D display positions to world positions such that they lie on an Image\+Actor. 

This Point\+Placer is used to constrain the placement of points on the supplied image actor. Additionally, you may set bounds to restrict the placement of the points. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} placement of points will then be constrained to lie not only on the Image\+Actor but also within the bounds specified. If no bounds are specified, they may lie anywhere on the supplied Image\+Actor.
