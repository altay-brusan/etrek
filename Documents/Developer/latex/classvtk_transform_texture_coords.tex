\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
transform (scale, rotate, translate) texture coordinates 

\doxylink{classvtk_transform_texture_coords}{vtk\+Transform\+Texture\+Coords} is a filter that operates on texture coordinates. It ingests any type of dataset, and outputs a dataset of the same type. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} filter lets you scale, translate, and rotate texture coordinates. For example, by using the Scale ivar, you can shift texture coordinates that range from (0-\/\texorpdfstring{$>$}{>}1) to range from (0-\/\texorpdfstring{$>$}{>}10) (useful for repeated patterns).

\doxylink{requirements_The}{The} filter operates on texture coordinates of dimension 1-\/\texorpdfstring{$>$}{>}3. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} texture coordinates are referred to as r-\/s-\/t. If the texture map is two dimensional, the t-\/coordinate (and operations on the t-\/coordinate) are ignored.
