\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Identifier within a \doxylink{classutil_1_1_name_space}{Name\+Space} for a local object. 

Local names are names which are directly accessible to and maintained by a \doxylink{classutil_1_1_name_space}{Name\+Space} within which they are local, indicated by the scope.
