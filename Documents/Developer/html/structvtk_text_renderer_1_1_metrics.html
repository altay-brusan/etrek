◆ Ascent

vtkVector2i vtkTextRenderer::Metrics::Ascent

Vectors representing the rotated ascent and descent of the text. This is the distance above or below the baseline. Not all backends support this, and may leave these vectors set to 0.

◆ BoundingBox

vtkTuple<int, 4> vtkTextRenderer::Metrics::BoundingBox

The axis-aligned bounding box of the rendered text and background, in pixels. The origin of the bounding box is the anchor point of the data when considering justification. Layout is { xMin, xMax, yMin, yMax }.
