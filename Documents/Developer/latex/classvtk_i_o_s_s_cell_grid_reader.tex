\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Reader for IOSS (Sierra IO System) that produces cell-\/grid data. 

This reader is a subclass of \doxylink{classvtk_i_o_s_s_reader}{vtk\+IOSSReader} that produces \doxylink{classvtk_cell_grid}{vtk\+Cell\+Grid} objects instead of \doxylink{classvtk_unstructured_grid}{vtk\+Unstructured\+Grid} objects inside each partition of its output partitioned-\/dataset collection.

This reader ignores some of its base-\/class settings:\+
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Remove\+Unused\+Points
\item Merge\+Exodus\+Entity\+Blocks
\end{DoxyItemize}
