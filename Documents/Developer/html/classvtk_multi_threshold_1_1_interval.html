int vtkMultiThreshold::Interval::Match ( double cellNorm[2])

Does the specified range fall inside the interval? For cell-centered attributes, only cellNorm[0] is examined. For point-centered attributes, cellNorm[0] is the minimum norm taken on over the cell and cellNorm[1] is the maximum.
