\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Display polar axes in Viewport 2D space. 

\doxylink{requirements_The}{The} polar axes actor is drawn on overlay. It displays polar coordinates. It is made of concentric axes linked with arcs.

\begin{DoxyWarning}{Warning}
Please be aware that the axes coordinate values are subject to perspective effects. With perspective projection, the computed distances may look wrong. These effects are not present when parallel projection is enabled. (
\end{DoxyWarning}

vtk\+Camera::\+\+Parallel\+Projection\+On())
