\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A one-\/dimensional coordinate system used to record the heights or depths of points. 

Such a coordinate system is usually dependent on the Earth\textquotesingle{}s gravity field. A \doxylink{classcs_1_1_vertical_c_s}{Vertical\+CS} shall have one axis association.
