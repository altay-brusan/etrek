Given a point x, a list of basis points p\+Ids, and a probability weighting function prob, compute interpolation weights associated with these basis points. Note that basis points list p\+Ids, the probability weighting prob, and the weights array are provided by the caller of the method, and may be dynamically resized as necessary. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} method returns the number of weights (p\+Ids may be resized in some cases). Typically this method is called after \doxylink{classvtk_generalized_kernel_a3c82d34597e534268c950babf870fbab}{Compute\+Basis()}, although advanced users can invoke \doxylink{classvtk_linear_kernel_abe113dab9038872e88e5b7780c03a300}{Compute\+Weights()} and provide the interpolation basis points p\+Ids directly. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} probably weighting prob are numbers 0\texorpdfstring{$<$}{<}=prob\texorpdfstring{$<$}{<}=1 which are multiplied against the interpolation weights before normalization. They are estimates of local confidence of weights. \doxylink{requirements_The}{The} prob may be nullptr in which all probabilities are considered =1.
