\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\doxylink{classvtk_glyph3_d}{vtk\+Glyph3D} on the GPU. 

Do the same job than \doxylink{classvtk_glyph3_d}{vtk\+Glyph3D} but on the GPU. For this reason, it is a mapper not a \doxylink{classvtk_poly_data_algorithm}{vtk\+Poly\+Data\+Algorithm}. Also, some methods of \doxylink{classvtk_glyph3_d}{vtk\+Glyph3D} don\textquotesingle{}t make sense in \doxylink{classvtk_glyph3_d_mapper}{vtk\+Glyph3\+DMapper}:\+ Generate\+Point\+Ids, old-\/style Set\+Source, Point\+Ids\+Name, Is\+Point\+Visible.
