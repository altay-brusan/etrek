64 * In general the memory allocation entry points are not kept
65 * thread specific but this can be overridden by LIBXML_THREAD_ALLOC_ENABLED

68 * - xmlRealloc
69 * - xmlMemStrdup
70 * - xmlFree

79 XML_OP(xmlMemStrdup, xmlStrdupFunc, XML_NO_ATTR)
