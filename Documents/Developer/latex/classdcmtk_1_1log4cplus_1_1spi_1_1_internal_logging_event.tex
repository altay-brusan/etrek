\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\doxylink{requirements_The}{The} internal representation of logging events. When an affirmative decision is made to log then a {\ttfamily \doxylink{classdcmtk_1_1log4cplus_1_1spi_1_1_internal_logging_event}{Internal\+Logging\+Event}} instance is created. This instance is passed around to the different log4cplus components.

This class is of concern to those wishing to extend log4cplus.
